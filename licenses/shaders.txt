slot's screen shaders are ports of shaders from the libretro shader collection
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders

LCD3x by Gigaherz. Public domain.

Dot by Themaister. Public domain.
Changes: sharper dots (falloff 4.0) and a 0.85 blend.

zfast_lcd_standard by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), copyright (C) 2017.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.

simpletex_lcd by jdgleaver, using the line weighting of zfast_lcd_standard.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
The paper texture (assets/paper.png, a 1024x1024 crop of textured_paper.png) is from the same shader.
Changes: the grid bias is 1.0, so black pixels carry no grid.

Authentic's Game Boy dot compositing, shadows and glass are adapted from
Gameboy Classic Shader v0.2.2 by Harlequin, copyright (C) 2013,
and its dmg-3x and gb-pocket-3x presets in the libretro shader collection.
GNU General Public License version 3 or later.
Changes: a single-pass dot and glass renderer, separate GPU frame response,
and a shadow cast from the selected bezel's outline.
Authentic's default bezels (assets/bezel_authentic_*.png) are cut from the same
presets' border art (handheld/console-border/resources, dmg-border-square-4x.png and
pocket-border-square-4x.png): the lens, the window's lip and shadow, the glass and the
power light, placed around slot's picture. Same licence.
Palette references: TWB64's DMG Ver. and Light Ver.; PixelShift's GB Old and
GB Pocket. GBC colour references: BGB's reality photographs and colours,
https://bgb.bircd.org/reality/ .
Measured GBA/GBC colour ramps and GBA response: Pokefan531's
Handheld-Colorspace-Shaders, https://github.com/Pokefan531/Handheld-Colorspace-Shaders .
