# Lid, Sleep and Power

## Closing the lid

Close the lid and the screen turns off and the game pauses. slot creates a save state and flushes your battery save to
disk. Open it again and you're back in the game.

Leave it shut and slot powers off after 3 minutes. Next time you power on, it boots straight back into that game.

Change the wait with Lid Power Off in Settings → System. You can pick 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.

## No lid?

On devices without a lid, tap <kbd>POWER</kbd> to sleep and tap it again to wake. The Lid Power Off timer works the same
way.

## Powering off

Hold <kbd>POWER</kbd> for a second. slot saves, then powers off.

When the battery hits 5%, and you're not charging, slot saves and powers off by itself so a dead battery can't ruin your day with lost progress.

## Restarting

Settings → System → About, then press <kbd>X</kbd>. Is this useful for anyone other than me? Probably not!

## The clock

The first time slot boots, or if the clock ever reads as long ago, it asks you to set the time. You can change it later
from Date & Time in Settings → System.

| Row   | Does                                            |
|-------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Date  | Month, day and year                             |
| Time  | Hour and minute                                 |
| City  | Your time zone, picked from a list of 50 cities |
| Clock | 12-Hour or 24-Hour                              |

Pick the city nearest you that shares your time zone. slot moves the clock for daylight saving on its own.

Games with a clock on the cart, like Pokémon Gold, Silver, Crystal, Ruby and Sapphire, use it.

## What about real deep sleep?

In my testing I've found the H700's deep sleep to be both unreliable and an energy hog.

Given that BaseOS boots in under 5 seconds I feel like this isn't a big loss.
